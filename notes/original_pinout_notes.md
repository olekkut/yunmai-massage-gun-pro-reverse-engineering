I will give you mode details that I have discovored:

on motor I see this pinout:

PWM : thick purple wire

GND : thick black wire

FR : thick yellow wire

FG : thick white  wire

VCC : thick red wire



on board I see

GND : thin red wire (goes to turn on/off switch)

KEY : thin black wire (goes to turn on/off switch)

8 : thin violet wire (goes to push button(tactile switch))

7 : thin orange wire (goes to push button(tactile switch))

6 : thin grey wire (goes to cathode of 1th smd led 3/3 lever indicator)

5 : thin brown wire (goes to cathode of 2th smd led 2/3 lever indicator)

4 : thin green wire (goes to cathode of 3th smd led 1/3 lever indicator)

3 : thin blue wire (goes to cathode of 4th smd led dual color indicator that lights white)

2 : thin white wire (goes to cathode of 4th smd led dual color indicator that lights red)

1 : thin yellow wire (seems that yellow goes to anode for all smd leds (1th,2th,3th,4th))

DC- : thick black wire (goes to charging port)

DC+ : thick black wire (goes to charging port)
